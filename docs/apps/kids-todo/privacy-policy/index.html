Kids Todo App

Who we are

Kids Todo App is a family task and reward app for parents or guardians and their children, published by Lorvium. A parent or guardian sets up the family, creates child profiles, and manages tasks, rewards, and settings. For privacy questions, contact privacy@lorvium.com .

Information we collect

Depending on how a family uses Kids Todo App, we may collect and process:

Account information
Email address, display name, authentication identifiers, sign-in provider details, and account settings.
Family and child profiles
Family membership, child display names or nicknames, avatars, optional age group, gender, parent or guardian relationship, and preferences. We ask for no more child information than the family features need.
Tasks, rewards, and coins
Chores, goals, schedules, completion status, coin balances and history, reward catalog items, claims, and approvals.
Uploaded images
Optional task-proof, reward, or support images that families choose to add. These files are stored in Firebase Storage.
Purchases and subscriptions
Subscription status and entitlements needed to provide premium features, prevent fraud, and support purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play under its own policies; we do not receive full payment card details.
Notifications
Push notification tokens and notification preferences used to send task, reward, account, and family activity reminders.
Diagnostics
Crash reports and diagnostics from Firebase Crashlytics (device model, app version, and crash data) to keep the app reliable. Optional product analytics stays disabled unless enabled for an app build and permitted by law and platform settings.
Security signals
Integrity and abuse-prevention signals from Firebase App Check and, on the web, reCAPTCHA or similar checks.
Support communications
Messages you send us and the information needed to investigate and resolve a request.

Sign-in providers

Kids Todo App may support Google Sign-In and other authentication providers for the parent account. When a parent signs in with a provider, we receive the information needed to authenticate the account, such as provider identifiers, email address, and basic profile details made available through the sign-in flow.

How we use information

We use information to operate Kids Todo App: to create and manage family spaces, show tasks and rewards, sync data across devices, send requested or necessary notifications, maintain purchases and subscriptions, provide support, improve reliability, diagnose crashes, secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Children's privacy and parent role

Kids Todo App is designed for parent-managed family use. A parent or guardian creates and manages the family account, supervises child use, configures tasks and rewards, and decides what child profile information is appropriate. Children should use the app only with a parent or guardian's permission and supervision, and are not asked to make privacy-sensitive choices on their own.

We do not knowingly collect more child information than is needed for the family task and reward features, and we do not sell children's data. Parents or guardians can request access, correction, export, or deletion using in-app controls where available or by contacting privacy@lorvium.com .

No ads or marketing trackers

Kids Todo App operates without third-party advertising networks or marketing trackers, and does not use the device advertising identifier. Children do not receive targeted advertising. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before any such technology is used, and any ads would be limited to parent-facing areas.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell family or child personal information. We use trusted service providers to host, secure, authenticate, and support the app, including Firebase and Google Cloud and the relevant app store, acting on our behalf and only as needed for the purposes described here. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and deletion

We keep account, family, task, reward, purchase, notification, image, diagnostic, and support information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Parents can delete their family or account data using in-app controls where available or by request. See the Data Deletion page for steps and details.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate for a family app, including authentication, authorization rules, Firebase security features, App Check, encryption in transit, and abuse-prevention controls. No service can guarantee perfect security, so keep account credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes or legal requirements evolve. Material updates are reflected by changing the "Last updated" date and, when appropriate, by notice in the app or by email.
